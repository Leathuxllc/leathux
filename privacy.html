Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LEATHUX ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our products sold on Amazon.com, this website, and the private software tools we use to manage our own Amazon seller and advertising accounts.

1. Who we are

LEATHUX is a brand of handmade leather goods. It is operated by an individual seller based in Pakistan and sells through Amazon.com. You can reach us at the contact details in section 9.

2. Information we collect

  • This website: we do not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or forms, and we do not collect personal information through this website. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard technical server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.
  • Amazon orders: Amazon processes all orders, payments and shipping (Fulfilled by Amazon). We do not receive customers' payment information. Amazon may share limited order information with us, such as the items ordered, as needed to run our business and provide customer support.
  • Messages you send us: if you contact us by email or through Amazon messaging, we receive the information you include in your message.

3. Amazon Selling Partner API and Amazon Ads API

We use a private, self-authorized application to access our own Amazon seller account data (through the Amazon Selling Partner API) and our own advertising account data (through the Amazon Ads API). We use this data only to manage our own business, including:

  • listings, pricing and inventory;
  • sales and advertising performance reporting;
  • managing and optimizing our own advertising campaigns.

The application is not offered to other sellers or advertisers. It does not access any other party's accounts, and it does not request customer personally identifiable information (PII) from Amazon.

4. How we use information

We use information only to fulfil orders and answer questions, to improve our products and listings, to manage our advertising, and to comply with legal obligations and Amazon's policies.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share information only with:

  • Amazon, as needed to operate our store;
  • service providers that help us run our business, such as software, data-analysis and AI tools, and only for that purpose;
  • authorities, where required by law.

6. Storage and security

API credentials and business data are stored privately and protected with reasonable technical measures. Access is limited to the account owner. Data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law or Amazon's policies.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us. Requests about Amazon account data, such as order history or payment details, should be made to Amazon directly.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last updated.
